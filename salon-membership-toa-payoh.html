Salon guide · 25 October 2026

Is a salon membership worth it in Toa Payoh?

Top Design Salon is at Lorong 7 Toa Payoh, #01-258, by Kim Keat Palm Market. Haircuts start from $28. The membership is stored value with bonus credit, and the site says it applies across salon services, from a trim to a full treatment.

The three cards, as published

  • Starter: top up $50, get $5 free, total value $55.
  • Popular: top up $100, get $15 free, total value $115.
  • Signature: top up $150, get $20 free, total value $170. This is marked as the best value of the three.

The bonus is the only discount the membership section states. A $28 haircut paid from the $55 card still costs a haircut. The extra value shows up because $50 of cash buys $55 of services, $100 buys $115, and $150 buys $170.

When paying per visit is the clearer choice

One haircut, or a single colour you are not sure you will repeat, does not need a card. Colouring starts from $80, treatments from $45, wash and blowdry from $18, and perm or rebonding from $120. If you already come often enough that those services add up past the top-up, the Signature card is the one the salon marks as the best of the three published options. If you might not be back, keep the money for the visit in front of you.

The cards and the service starting prices are on the home page. Questions about a visit go through the contact page or the FAQ. The store number published for the salon is 6909 8342.
