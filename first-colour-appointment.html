Colour guide · 26 October 2026

Your first colour appointment

Signature Colour Artistry at Top Design Salon starts from $80. The studio describes the shades as selected for your skin tone and hair integrity, and designed to grow out gracefully between visits. The salon is at Lor 7 Toa Payoh, #01-258, Singapore 310018. The phone is 6909 8342.

From $80 means a starting price

The homepage states colour from $80. It does not print a single price for highlights, a colour correction, or very long hair. Ask for the figure before the colour is mixed. A cut, if you want one the same day, is Precision Architecture & Styling from $28 and is a separate line.

What to bring

  • One photo of a colour you want, taken in daylight.
  • A note of box dye, bleach, or a perm in the last couple of years.
  • How often you can come back. A shade that grows out between visits is the brief the salon already uses.

This page does not describe a mixing method. The consultation is where the stylist says whether the photo is possible on your hair that day. Perming and rebonding start from $120 and are not part of a colour booking unless you ask for them.
